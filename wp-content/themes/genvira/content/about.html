OUR COMPANY

From research roots
to therapeutic development.

Genvira is an Ottawa-based biotechnology company focused on viral-vector engineering.

Spun out of the Ottawa Hospital Research Institute in 2019, the company brings scientific and translational experience to the design of viral-based therapeutics.

Our focus today is advancing GVB-2030 for bladder cancer while developing a broader pipeline and working with partners.

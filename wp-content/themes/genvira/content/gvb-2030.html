LEAD PROGRAM

Engineering a vector
with a therapeutic purpose.

GVB-2030 is an investigational, engineered vaccinia virus program for bladder cancer.

The program is designed to carry cytokine payloads and engage the tumor environment. Our development work focuses on the vector, its biological activity, and the evidence needed to support further clinical development.

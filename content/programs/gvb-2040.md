## A cancer vaccine tailored to the patient

GVB-2040 is Genvira’s next therapeutic development focus after [GVB-2030](@/programs/gvb-2030.md).

Both programs aim to stimulate an immune response against cancer. GVB-2040 tailors the vaccine to each patient’s human leukocyte antigen (HLA) profile.

---

- **Program**: GVB-2040
- **Approach**: Personalized cancer vaccine
- **Personalization**: Patient HLA profile
- **Published stage**: Preclinical

---

## Why HLA matters

A patient’s HLA profile helps determine which tumor antigens their immune system can recognize.

---

HLA personalization graphic placeholder

---

### Antigen presentation

HLA molecules display protein fragments on the surface of cells for T cells to inspect.

### Patient variation

HLA varies from person to person, so the same tumor antigen may be presented differently across patients.

### Published research

A separate personalized cancer vaccine study observed T-cell responses against selected tumor antigens in patients with gastrointestinal cancers. [Read the study](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/134915)

---

## From preclinical research toward clinical evaluation

GVB-2040 is in preclinical development, with early cell-line data.

---

### Early cell-line studies

Initial data come from laboratory studies in cells.

### Preclinical development

The program is focused on evaluating the HLA-personalized vaccine approach.

### Future clinical studies

Clinical studies would assess safety and potential benefit in patients.

---

## How sequencing informs personalization

---

### Profile the patient

Deep sequencing of a patient sample informs the HLA profile.

### Identify candidate tumor antigens

Deep sequencing of the cancer biopsy helps identify candidate antigens.

### Tailor the vaccine

Together, these data inform which tumor antigens to include in a vaccine tailored to the patient.

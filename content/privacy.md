## When you visit the site

You can browse the public website without creating an account. The site does not use advertising trackers or visitor analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## When you contact us

If you email or call us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to share. We use that information to respond and follow up. If you send a cover letter or résumé, we use it to consider your career inquiry. Please leave medical records and other sensitive information out of general inquiries.

## Maps and external links

The map on our [Contact page](@/contact.md) loads map images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation. The foundation may receive your IP address and details about the request. See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). If you follow a link to Google Maps, a journal, or another website, that service’s privacy practices apply.

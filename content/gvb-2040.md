## A cancer vaccine tailored to the patient

GVB-2040 is Genvira’s next therapeutic development focus after [GVB-2030](@/gvb-2030.md). Both programs aim to stimulate an immune response against cancer. GVB-2040 adds a patient-specific step by tailoring the vaccine to each patient’s human leukocyte antigen (HLA) profile.

## Why HLA matters

HLA molecules display protein fragments on the surface of cells for T cells to inspect. HLA varies from person to person, so the same tumor antigen may be presented differently across patients. GVB-2040 puts that variation at the center of vaccine design, with the goal of directing a T-cell response against tumor antigens the patient’s immune system can recognize.

## How sequencing informs personalization

GVB-2040’s approach uses deep sequencing of a patient sample and a cancer biopsy. The patient sample informs the HLA profile, while the biopsy helps identify candidate tumor antigens. Together, these data inform which tumor antigens to include in a vaccine tailored to the patient.

In published research on a separate personalized cancer vaccine, investigators observed vaccine-induced T-cell responses against selected tumor antigens in patients with gastrointestinal cancers. [Published study in the Journal of Clinical Investigation](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/134915)

## Development stage

GVB-2040 is preclinical, with early cell-line data. Future studies will need to establish the program’s safety and assess its potential benefit in patients.

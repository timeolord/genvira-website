## Clinical development progress

A partnered Phase 1 study evaluated a Genvira-engineered vector in a separate development program. We reported encouraging preliminary safety and efficacy signals from that study.

[GVB-2030](@/gvb-2030.md), our lead program for bladder cancer, is in IND-enabling studies. Our next goal is first-in-human evaluation. We are also developing the preclinical programs listed above.

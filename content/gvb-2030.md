## Engineering a vector with a therapeutic purpose.

GVB-2030 is an investigational, engineered vaccinia virus program for bladder cancer.

The program is designed to carry cytokine payloads and engage the tumor environment. Our development work focuses on the vector, its biological activity, and the evidence needed to support further clinical development.

---

- **Program**: GVB-2030
- **Indication**: Bladder cancer
- **Vector**: Engineered vaccinia virus
- **Payload class**: Cytokines
- **Published stage**: IND-enabling studies

### Beyond stages 0 and I

Stage 0 disease is confined to the lining. In stage I, the tumor has entered connective tissue but has not reached the muscle. Stage II disease involves the muscle layer; the illustration shows that deeper invasion.

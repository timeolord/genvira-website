# CG Oncology and Arrakis reference review

Reviewed 12 November 2026. This review covers the primary corporate navigation, linked page sections, research indexes, and representative program and news detail pages. The primary pages were reviewed through text and image descriptions. The desktop homepages were subsequently loaded and visually inspected in Chromium. Detailed animation timing, hover behavior, quantitative contrast, and competitor mobile rendering were not audited. Historical releases, publication files, vacancies, and financial filings were not exhaustively reviewed. Both sitemap URLs were inaccessible through the research tool.

## Decisions for the prototype

Lead with GVB-2030 and use separate pages for program details, pipeline, and platform. Provide a smaller publication and news section with dates and sources. Keep the SARS-CoV-2 pseudovirus offering on a secondary research tools page. Use the supplied Genvira branding rather than either competitor's assets or palette.

Follow the email's preference for immediate, selectable text and useful scientific diagrams. Essential content should be visible without waiting for animations and available without video or audio. Treat clinical stage, trial status, and performance claims as company content requiring verification, never as decorative placeholders.

## Desktop visual follow-up

CG Oncology uses a dark blue hero, a bladder illustration, strong typography, orange accents, and a clear pipeline call to action. Genvira carries over the direct hierarchy and separated illustration, with its own cyan logo palette and readable white text on a solid background.

Arrakis uses a magenta and orange introductory field and animated headline reveals. Its page text was available before all headline lines appeared in the captured viewport. Genvira displays its essential copy immediately and avoids those reveal sequences.

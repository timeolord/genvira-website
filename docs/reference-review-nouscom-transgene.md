# Nouscom and Transgene reference review

Reviewed 12 November 2026 for the Genvira prototype. Nouscom's main navigation pages and news category landing pages were accessible. Transgene initially returned HTTP 403 through text retrieval, but its main corporate and program pages subsequently loaded with HTTP 200 in Chromium. The review below uses that browser navigation and page content, supplemented by official indexed content. A desktop homepage screenshot was inspected; the remaining pages were reviewed through their rendered document text and headings. Sitemap retrieval was unavailable. This is not an exhaustive mobile or animation audit, and does not cover every historical article, archive page, or PDF.

The News navigation entry resolves to the homepage; the five categories above provide the actual archives. Representative details reviewed include a [publication entry](https://nouscom.com/2026/01/16/nous-209-neoantigen-vaccine-for-cancer-prevention-in-lynch-syndrome-carriers-a-phase-1b-2-trial/) with an external article link and a [leadership announcement](https://nouscom.com/2026/11/02/nouscom-strengthens-leadership-and-operational-capabilities-appointing-loredana-siani-ph-d-as-chief-technology-officer/) using a dated article format.

Borrow the clear technology/program distinction and the references supporting scientific statements. Avoid the image-only pipeline: it makes important development information harder to access on mobile or through assistive tools. Parsed pages explicitly expose a content-protection message, consistent with the email's concern about text selection. Genvira should preserve ordinary selection, copying, and browser behavior.

## Transgene

The live header has five main groups: About us, myvac, Pipeline, Investors, and Career. About contains leadership, board, advisors, governance, and ESG links. Pipeline contains individual program pages, publications, and a clinical FAQ. Investors contains presentation/contact, events, financial, stock, and shareholder-meeting sections. The footer adds Partnering, Suppliers, Contacts, and policy pages.

Borrow the named-program hierarchy, consistent program resources, publication filters, and clear partner contact route. Avoid making video the main explanation: the homepage's large embedded video takes considerable space before the lead program appears. Some program media also explicitly requires analytics cookies; Genvira's essential explanations should work without media playback or optional cookies. Ensure navigation preserves the selected language.

Older indexed pages such as the [TG4050 announcement](https://www.transgene.com/annonce-tg4050en/) and [Therapeutic vaccines](https://www.transgene.com/therapeutic-vaccines/) retain older development descriptions. These legacy examples were reviewed through official indexed content, rather than the browser pass. Genvira should keep one canonical page per program and date its evidence, reducing the risk of conflicting claims across old campaign pages.

## Application to Genvira

Lead with GVB-2030 and a plain-language introduction, followed by direct links to its program page and pipeline. Separate program information from platform engineering. Put supporting publications beside relevant statements, and use a single Research & Updates section until there is enough content to justify separate archives. Describe the science through readable text and diagrams; do not require video or audio to understand it.

Competitor examples inform structure, not Genvira's scientific claims. Their trial stages, results, regulatory status, and manufacturing claims must not be transferred to Genvira copy.
